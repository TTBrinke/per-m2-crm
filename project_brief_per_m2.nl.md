# Per-M2.nl CRM & Project System

## Brand Identity
- **Colors**: Stone (neutral base), Amber/Gold (brand color), Emerald (success/money).
- **Typography**: Inter (sans-serif), bold weights for headings and numbers.
- **Tone**: Professional, data-driven, reliable, yet modern and sleek.

## Key Screens to Generate
1. **Dashboard**: High-level overview of revenue, margin, and active leads.
2. **Relaties (Contacts)**: Detailed table/list of customers with contact info.
3. **Verkoopkansen (Sales Opportunities)**: Tracking leads from initial contact to quote.
4. **Projecten (Projects)**: Project management view with task tracking and planning.
5. **Producten (Product Catalog)**: CRUD management for PVC products and services.
6. **Facturen (Invoices)**: Billing history and status tracking.

## Core Interactive Elements
- **PVC Calculator**: Precision tool for calculating square meters with cutting loss.
- **Offerte Editor**: Real-time quote builder with article search.
- **Print Previews**: Styled documents for quotes, orders, and invoices.
- **Quick Add**: Modal-driven entry for new contacts and leads.
